FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What makes OwlCalc different from standard mobile calculators?

OwlCalc combines a high-contrast grid layout with a high-precision math engine (accurate to 10 decimal places, no floating-point rounding), reactive dependency canvas tape (where past calculation edits auto-update downstream values), and tap-to-insert interop between tools.

How does Tap-to-Insert Interop work?

When you perform a calculation in one calculator (e.g. Basic Math or Currency), switching to another calculator (e.g. Tip, Discount, or BMI) automatically displays a button at the top allowing you to insert the previous result with a single tap.

Is my calculation history stored locally?

Yes! 100% of your calculation tape is stored locally on your device (using browser localStorage on web and SQLite on mobile). Your financial and personal data never leaves your device.
