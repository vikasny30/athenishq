Capabilities

What each product actually does.

Aletheia
  • Detects 9 distinct behavioral signatures in LLM output — confidence without grounding, scope creep, credibility exploitation, and more
  • Every signature validated against 2,571 real-world incidents from AIID, AVID, and the MIT AI Risk Repository
  • Public benchmark scores and a live playground — nothing is a private claim
OwlScore
  • Scores the S&P 100 nightly and hash-chains every score to a public ledger before the outcome is known
  • 43.6% historical backtest accuracy, tracked and published, not cherry-picked
  • 59 live forward predictions currently open — screener, watchlist, and full track record are public
OwlNote
  • Freeform Apple Pencil canvas built on a Skia render engine, tuned for iPad and iPhone
  • An AI assistant that reads handwritten strokes directly — no forced transcription step
  • Folders, direct drag-to-move, and per-note typography themes
CAL-C
  • 18+ specialized calculator tools beyond arithmetic — unit conversion, finance, statistics
  • Math held to 10 decimal places on every operation, not floating-point approximation
  • A reactive tape history you can edit and recompute from any prior step
TireScan
  • Reads tread depth and wear pattern from a single photo, no special equipment
  • Flags uneven wear that usually indicates an alignment or suspension issue

Studio

Five products deep, two shipped and live, before the first outside hire.

AthenisHQ is led by a founding team of engineering leaders with over a decade building AI-powered platforms and infrastructure at internet scale — leading teams that ship products used by hundreds of millions of people, and building the deployment and reliability systems that keep them running.

That's the bar every product here is held to: production-grade, at scale, no shortcuts. Two are already live proving it end-to-end; the rest are close behind, and the studio is now building the team to take all five further.

None of the five compete on being first — they compete on being verifiably right: a public hash-chained ledger instead of a claimed track record, empirical failure rates instead of vibes-based safety claims.

Monetization isn't theoretical, either — it's already built into each product: Aletheia is source-available with paid commercial licensing for hosted use, OwlScore stays free with full S&P 500 coverage planned as a premium tier, and OwlNote and CAL-C both ship free-to-start with a Pro subscription unlocking sync and AI.
