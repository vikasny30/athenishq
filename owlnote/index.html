FAQ

Good to know

Is OwlNote out yet?

Not yet — it's in active development. Join the waitlist and you'll hear the moment it ships.

Does it work without an Apple Pencil?

The canvas is designed Pencil-first for the most natural feel, though finger input works too.

Where does the AI assistant get its answers?

Directly from your own notes — it reads your handwriting on-device to answer questions and summarize, nothing else.

What happens to my notes if I delete them by accident?

They sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days before being permanently removed, so there's always a way back.
